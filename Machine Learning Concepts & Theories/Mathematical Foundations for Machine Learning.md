# 5. Mathematical Foundations for Machine Learning

Mathematics provides the theoretical foundation for understanding how Machine Learning algorithms work.

## Main Mathematical Topics

### 1. Linear Algebra
The study of vectors, matrices, and mathematical operations used in Machine Learning.

### 2. Calculus
The study of derivatives, gradients, and optimization techniques used to minimize errors during model training.

### 3. Probability
The study of uncertainty and the likelihood of events.

### 4. Statistics
The study of collecting, analyzing, interpreting, and understanding data.

## Why Mathematics is Important in ML?

- Understanding how algorithms work.
- Understanding model optimization.
- Analyzing data and identifying patterns.
- Improving model performance.
